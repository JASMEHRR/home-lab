# Posture Coach: decisions

- **MediaPipe Pose + Face Landmarker (lite models)** for body points and face. Free, runs on the laptop CPU, no cloud. Installed with pip into `C:\Users\jasme\posture-pylibs` (separate from `eyetoy-pylibs`, because MediaPipe brings its own OpenCV and must not replace the camera program's). Undo: delete that folder. Models live in `posture\models` (downloaded from Google's MediaPipe model storage, 2026-11-12: pose 5.5 MB, face 3.6 MB).
- **3D posture, not flat angles.** The first version measured the ear-to-shoulder angle in the picture. That only works from a true side view, and the EyeToy sees a diagonal, so it judged wrong. Now the head-forward angle comes from MediaPipe's 3D body points, which gave the same number from both cameras within a few degrees in live tests.
- **Shoulder tilt only from the front camera.** From the side the shoulders overlap and the 3D guess was wrong (20+ degrees from the EyeToy while the webcam saw 3). This was the main source of false "bad posture" in v1.
- **Each camera is measured against its own calibrated "sitting straight" value.** Calibrate button: sit up straight, look at the screen. Saved in `calibration.json`. Without it, defaults are used and lean-in is not judged.
- **Face for focus, from the laptop webcam only:** eyes closed (blink score), head turn (yaw), head tilt down (pitch), eyes down. Looking at the screen from a webcam above it already means looking slightly down, so limits are measured from the calibrated look-at-screen values. A turned head the face model cannot see counts as "looking away".
- **ASSUMPTION (unverified on a real head):** MediaPipe's face matrix uses y up, so a head tilted down makes the third column's y negative (`metrics.head_angles`). Unit tests cover the maths; only a real look-down test confirms the sign. If "Looking down" never triggers when you look at your lap, flip the sign of `pitch_down` there.
- **Sensitivity** Relaxed/Normal/Strict = 18/12/8 degrees of forward head beyond your calibrated posture. Sleepy alert at most once per 10 minutes.
- **Glow stays on while posture is bad** (v3). The old rule (60 continuous bad seconds, once per 5 minutes) never fired because bad spells were under 15 s, so the glow was never seen. Now it starts after 3 s of bad posture (setting `glow_after`), stays until you sit right, then fades, with a beep every 8 s.
- **Activity labels, studying vs not.** Studying = looking at the screen, or head down with no phone (writing in a notebook; the webcam sits above the screen, so writing looks like "looking down"). Not studying = phone in hand, sitting back from the screen (face under 82% of its calibrated size), looking away, sleepy. All of it is in the CSV (`focus`, new `studying` column) and the Today tab. While writing, 15 more degrees of forward head is allowed and "leaning in" is not judged.
- **Phone detection:** MediaPipe EfficientDet-Lite0 (`models\efficientdet_lite0.tflite`, 6.9 MB, Google model storage) finds "cell phone" in both cameras; it counts only when a wrist or finger point is next to it, so a phone lying on the desk does not. UNVERIFIED with a real phone in hand: try it and tell me if it misses.
- **Without calibration** the screen distance is learned from the first 40 face readings of the session, so "sitting back" works from the first minute. Calibrate for better numbers.
- **Old CSV files:** a file from an older version gets the new column added to its header; rows from before have no `studying` value and the Today tab works it out from `focus` (old `looking_down` counts as writing).
- **Numbers only, never video**, one CSV row per second in `data\posture-YYYY-MM-DD.csv`. Video is saved only when you press Record (both cameras side by side, `captures\coach_*.mp4`). First-version test data moved to `data\old-v1` (different columns).
- **Glow alert:** a see-through, click-through layer over the primary screen (`overlay.py`). It reads "Jarvis lighting" as an on-screen glow; the physical LED light was not touched.
- **App is tkinter** (already installed; nothing new to download). The old HTML report is still available from the Today tab.
- **The old `posture_tracker.py` is now a launcher** for `app.py`, so a taskbar icon pinned earlier still works.
- **Study time is not in here.** The tracker knows you are at the desk and looking at the screen, not what is on it. ActivityWatch (verified installer in `Downloads\activitywatch-dl`) would add which apps and sites; Windows blocked running it automatically, so it needs the user to run it.
